Emergency notice

KinVault support is not monitored for emergencies. If someone may be in danger or needs urgent medical help, contact local emergency services immediately.

Before you contact us

Reminders do not appear

Check notification permission, focus/do-not-disturb settings, battery restrictions, and whether the reminder is enabled inside KinVault.

Family alerts do not arrive

Check internet access, Firebase/push notification permission, caregiver pairing, and whether monitoring is paused or snoozed.

Caregiver sees stale status

Open KinVault on both devices, confirm the devices are paired, and check whether relay sync is pending.

Subscription issue

Purchases, renewals, cancellations, and refunds are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. Use the store account that purchased Pro.

Feedback inside the app

KinVault may include an in-app feedback form. If you do not enter an email address, the app may send a placeholder email value so the support system can accept the message. Please avoid sending medical emergency details, full medical records, government IDs, payment card details, or private notes that are not needed for support.
