Privacy Terms Support

1. Overview

BabyDays is a baby care tracking app for logging feeding, sleep, diaper changes, growth, milestones, medicine notes, handoff notes, and parent check-ins. We design BabyDays to be calm, privacy-conscious, and useful at tired moments.

Important: BabyDays is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or emergency advice.

2. Information you may enter

  • Baby profile: baby name, birth date, and optional profile details.
  • Care logs: feeding, sleep, diaper, growth, medicine, milestone, handoff, and note entries.
  • Parent check-ins: mood and energy entries that you choose to record.
  • Settings: language, unit preferences, dark mode, reminders, and premium entitlement state.
  • Support emails: if you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and any details you include.

3. Local-first storage

BabyDays stores care data primarily on your device using local storage. In the current offline-first design, your baby logs, parent check-ins, notes, and milestones are not uploaded to a BabyDays server by default.

If you share, export, email, save to Files, or open content in another app, the selected third-party app or service may receive the data you choose to share.

4. Notifications

BabyDays may ask permission to schedule local reminders, such as a next feeding reminder. Local notifications are scheduled on your device. Remote notifications may be added only if cloud/family sync features are introduced and tested later.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Premium purchases are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. We may store a local premium entitlement flag so the app can unlock purchased features.

6. Analytics, diagnostics, and Firebase

BabyDays may use Firebase or similar services for analytics, crash diagnostics, and messaging if enabled in a production build. These services may process technical information such as device type, OS version, app version, crash logs, event names, and notification tokens. We do not intentionally upload your local baby care logs or parent check-in content to analytics.

7. No ads and no sale of data

BabyDays is designed with no ads. We do not sell your personal data.

8. Children and family data

BabyDays is intended for parents, guardians, and caregivers. Because baby and family care data can be sensitive, please avoid entering information you do not want stored on the device or shared through exports.

9. Data deletion

You can remove app data by deleting logs where available, clearing app data through your device settings, or uninstalling the app. If you contacted us by email, you can request deletion of support correspondence where legally possible.

10. International processing

Support email providers, Apple, Google, Firebase, and other service providers may process technical or support information in countries other than where you live.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when BabyDays changes. The updated date at the top shows the latest version.
