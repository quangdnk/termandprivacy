1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how ScanSpace ("ScanSpace", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the App. ScanSpace is a mobile utility app for scanning QR codes, creating QR codes, scanning documents, editing PDFs, organizing history, and using related PDF tools.

We designed ScanSpace to be local-first. Scans, generated QR content, PDFs, drafts, folders, and history are stored on your device unless you choose to share, export, open, or send them using another service.

2. Information You Provide or Create

The App may process the following information so it can provide its features:

  • QR scan results: URLs, Wi-Fi details, text, phone numbers, email links, Secure QR payloads, or PDF QR payloads that you scan.
  • QR content you create: URLs, Wi-Fi details, contacts, text, emails, event details, social usernames, Secure QR content, and other QR payload data entered by you.
  • Documents and PDFs: images, imported files, scanned document pages, edited PDF files, annotations, stamps, signatures, watermarks, passwords, OCR text, and exported files.
  • History, folders, and drafts: saved scan items, created QR items, PDF edit drafts, document scan drafts, favorite markers, folders, and usage organization data.
  • Feedback: if you submit feedback by email, we may receive your email address, message, device-provided email metadata, and any information you choose to include.

This information is generally stored locally on your device. We do not operate a cloud sync service for ScanSpace in version 1 unless a future feature explicitly states otherwise.

3. Permissions

Depending on the features you use, the App may request access to:

  • Camera: to scan QR codes and capture document pages.
  • Photos or media library: to import images for QR analysis or document/PDF creation.
  • Files and document picker: to import, edit, export, or share PDFs and related files.
  • Notifications: to receive app messages, campaign alerts, or product updates if enabled.

You can manage these permissions in your device settings.

4. Analytics, Diagnostics, and Notifications

ScanSpace may use Firebase services to improve reliability and product quality:

  • Firebase Analytics: helps us understand high-level app usage, such as feature usage and app interactions.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: helps us diagnose crashes, errors, device state, app version, operating system version, and technical logs related to failures.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: may process a device push token to deliver notifications or remote campaigns when notifications are enabled.
  • Firebase Remote Config: may be used to control app messages, campaigns, paywall copy, or feature flags.

These services may process device identifiers, app instance identifiers, diagnostics, usage data, and technical metadata. They do not require us to upload your scanned PDFs, created QR content, scan history, or local drafts.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

ScanSpace may offer free features, Pro features, subscriptions, free trials, lifetime purchases, or temporary unlocks. Payments are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your full credit card number.

We may receive purchase status information, product identifiers, entitlement status, transaction identifiers, or subscription state from Apple, Google, or in-app purchase SDKs so the App can unlock Pro features.

6. Sharing and Exporting

When you use actions such as Share, Open link, Copy, Export PDF, Export DOCX, Save to Files, or send feedback, information may be sent to third-party apps or services selected by you, such as email apps, cloud storage, browsers, messaging apps, or file providers. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

7. Secure QR and PDF Passwords

Secure QR features are intended to protect QR content with local encryption. PDF password features are intended to protect PDF files. You are responsible for remembering passwords. We do not operate a password recovery service for local Secure QR or protected PDF files.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it, clear relevant history or drafts, remove files, or uninstall the App. App analytics and diagnostic data may be retained by Firebase or related service providers according to their retention settings and policies.

If you contact us by email, we may retain that conversation for support, product improvement, and abuse prevention.

9. Children

ScanSpace is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

10. International Users

ScanSpace may be used globally. Service providers such as Firebase, Apple, Google, email providers, and analytics or diagnostic systems may process information in countries other than where you live. We take a data-minimizing approach and avoid uploading your local scan content, PDFs, or drafts unless you choose to share or export them.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date and may provide additional notice in the App where appropriate.
