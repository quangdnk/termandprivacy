Emergency Privacy

Tap the Emergency Privacy button to instantly hide all data. Your logs are not deleted.

What to include

  • Your device model and iOS or Android version.
  • The CycleLog app version if you know it.
  • A short description of the problem or request.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue, if possible.
  • Screenshots only if you are comfortable sharing them.

Medical disclaimer

CycleLog provides cycle estimates only. It is not medical advice and not a contraceptive method. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical guidance related to your menstrual or reproductive health.

Legal and privacy

You can review the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. CycleLog is designed as a fully offline, privacy-first app. Your cycle data stays on your device and is never uploaded to external servers.
